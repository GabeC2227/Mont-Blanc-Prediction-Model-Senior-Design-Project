# Channel-only glacier experiment

Restored the earlier narrow glacier-channel overlay. The existing regional
annual forecast controls illustrative thinning through the year slider.
The terrain texture is unchanged. There is no whole-terrain snow-color effect
and no multi-glacier footprint overlay. glacierFootprints.json is not used.

The footprint, starting thickness and thinning scale are illustrative.
The module samples the terrain through a spatial index to keep startup fast.
The existing main.js imports and slider connection remain compatible.
