How to Use the Model

While the simulation loads, please read up on how to understand and interact with the model!

There are two visualization methods: Ice Cover and Severity Markers. You can switch between both methods or only use one.

The Ice Cover method showcases the decline of glaciers on the mountain throughout time.

The Severity Markers method doesn’t showcase the decline in the same way. It uses colored dots to show how extreme melting was in that area in that year. Here is the key to follow it:

🔵 Blue
🟡 Yellow
🟠 Orange
🔴 Red - Extreme Melting
